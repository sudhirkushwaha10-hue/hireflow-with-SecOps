Topic : DevSecOps

what is DevSecOps : DevSecOps means interating securrity controls throughtout the software development lifecycle (SDLC) instead of treating security as a final manual stage.
The goal is to detect issues early, automate security checks, enforce policies in CI/CD and continuously monitoring production.
